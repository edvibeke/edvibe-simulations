How to use: Drag the Energy added slider up slowly, or click Auto-heat. Watch the particles in the box — they start locked in a lattice, then slip past each other, then fly apart. Watch the thermometer too. You may be surprised: it doesn't always rise.
💡 Notice: at 0 °C and again at 100 °C, the temperature stops rising even though you keep adding energy. The energy is being used to break the bonds between particles, not to make them move faster. Only once the change is complete does the temperature rise again.
