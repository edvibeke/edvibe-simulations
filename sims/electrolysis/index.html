How to use: Click Switch on power. The battery pushes electrons through the wires, making the left electrode negative (the cathode) and the right electrode positive (the anode). Blue Cu²⁺ ions drift to the cathode and turn into copper metal. Orange SO₄²⁻ ions drift to the anode where oxygen bubbles form. Watch the blue colour fade as Cu²⁺ ions leave the solution.
Notice: the solution fades from blue to colourless. That's because Cu²⁺ ions are being removed from the solution — they gain electrons at the cathode and become solid copper. Meanwhile oxygen bubbles form at the anode. The sulfate ions don't change — they're just spectators. This is how copper is purified in industry.
