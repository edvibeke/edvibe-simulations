How to use: Beetles come in many shades of green. Change the background colour so it's lighter or darker, and turn up the predator pressure. Beetles that stand out get eaten more easily; ones that blend in survive and reproduce. The top graph shows the spread of colours, the bottom one the average colour over generations.
💡 Notice: individuals don't change colour — but the population does. Over time the average colour moves toward the background. The variation is already there; the predator just picks which variants survive. That's natural selection: it doesn't invent traits, it sorts the ones that already exist.
