How to use: Move the sliders to set launch angle and speed. Press Fire to launch. The trajectory of your previous shot stays on screen as a dashed line so you can compare. Try keeping speed the same and changing only the angle — what angle gives the longest range?
Notice: 30° and 60° travel the same distance. 45° goes furthest. Angles that add up to 90° give the same range.
