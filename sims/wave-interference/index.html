How to use: Drag anywhere on the canvas to move the probe. The readout shows how far the probe is from each source, and the difference in wavelengths. When the path difference is a whole number (0, 1, 2…), the waves arrive in step and reinforce. When it's a half-number (0.5, 1.5…), they cancel. This is exactly what happens in a ripple tank or a double-slit experiment — bright fringes are constructive, dark bands are destructive.
💡 Notice: on the midline between the sources, path difference is always zero — the waves arrive together. That vertical line of brightness is a permanent constructive ridge.
