How to use: A ⇌ B is a reversible reaction. Blue A particles turn into green B, and green B turn back into blue A. The particles never stop reacting — they just reach a balance where the two rates are equal. Try the buttons: add more A, add more B, or change the temperature. Watch the graph shift, then settle again at a new balance.
💡 Notice: the reactions never stop — this is dynamic equilibrium. When you disturb the balance, the system shifts to oppose your change: add more A → it makes more B. Heat it up → the endothermic direction speeds up more, shifting the balance. That's Le Chatelier's principle.
